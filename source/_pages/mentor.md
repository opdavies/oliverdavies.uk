I've mentored people at events like DrupalCamps and DrupalCons to become new core contributors, and for bootcamps like School of Code and DevelopMe\_ to help people learn software development and find their first roles.

I'm currently offering free 1-to-1 online mentoring sessions, particularly for those who are looking for their first role in software development.

## Interested?

[Contact me and apply](mailto:oliver+mentoring@oliverdavies.uk) by telling me about yourself, your experience and objectives, and how you think I can help.

Sessions will only be available to a small number of people at a time, and I will start a waitlist if I need to manage demand.

These are **self-guided** mentoring sessions, and not personalised coaching. I won't be teaching or telling you what to learn, but I will help you learn what you want.

## How does it work?

If I think I can help, I'll contact you to book a short introduction call. If we decide to proceed, you can book a weekly 30-minute mentoring call with me.

You can also contact me via email, although I don't guarantee any specific response times but will respond as soon as possible.

## What can I help with?

Here are some things I use and do, and can help you with:

- PHP
- Drupal
- Symfony and Laravel
- JavaScript and TypeScript
- Front-end development and accessibility
- Linux
- Nix
- DevOps, system administration, and automation

## Get in touch

If I can help you with any of these things, please [get in touch](mailto:oliver+mentoring@oliverdavies.uk) and let's talk.
