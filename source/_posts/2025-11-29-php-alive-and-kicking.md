A few weeks ago, I was interviewed by Chris and Mike from [PHP Architect][0] for the "PHP Alive and Kicking" podcast and the episode is now live.

Our original plan was to talk about Drupal, and we did get there after discussing other topics such as mentoring, coding bootcamps and getting Junior Developers into the industry.

I'll embed the video below, or you can watch it and the other 17 episodes on the [PHP Architect YouTube channel][1].

This episode is also why I was at [the TechMids conference][2] and got to give [a presentation on Nix][3], whilst getting to see Chris and Mike in person.

I'm looking forward to doing more podcasts and public speaking next year, and will post any announcements here.
