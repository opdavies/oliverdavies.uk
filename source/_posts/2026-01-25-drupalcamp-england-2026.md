[DrupalCamp England](https://www.drupalcampengland.org) returns next month, at the University of Salford from the 28th February until the 1st of March.

[Tickets](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/drupalcamp-england-2026-tickets-1977894114540) and [sponsorship opportunities](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oQ1Gnev8eWi_1RZJDK64Ddl7GyHuaTgdpnNK4290U1I/edit?usp=sharing) are available, and [session submissions are open](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeaaUv3HeJcz24IQODgf_WU0PQ7u00v-vkrZ78WGMJjKVLKvw/viewform) until the 31st of January.
