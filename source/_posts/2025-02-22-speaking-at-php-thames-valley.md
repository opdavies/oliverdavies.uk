After speaking at PHP Oxford last January, I'm happy to be going back to Oxford to give my first presentation in 2025 at the renamed PHP Thames Valley meetup.

The event is the 20th of March and I'll be speaking about Sculpin - the PHP static website generator that I use for my personal website.

I'll explain what static site generators are, why I like them and some of Sculpin's core concepts before jumping into a live demo.

[RSVPs are open][0] for the event and I'd love to see you there!
