I'm a long-time user of the [Sculpin static site generator][0].

I created a [Sculpin skeleton project][1] in November 2014 and used it to [rebuild my website][2] in March 2015.

It was a way for me to easily [learn and use Twig][3] before Drupal 8's release, and I've given Sculpin presentations at meetups and conferences - including [Valentine's Day 2024][5] at PHP South West in Bristol.

I've used it for client projects and DrupalCamp websites, and it was one of the things I showed to 40 BTEC Computing students at [Reading College][6].

I'm happy to say I'm now a contributor to the project after two pull requests I submitted were approved and merged.

Some [code style issues][7] are fixed, and Sculpin now [has a Nix flake][8] for local development that creates a reproducible environment for all contributors.

I'd like to do more open source contributions in 2026, and Sculpin is a project I'd definitely like to contribute to again.
