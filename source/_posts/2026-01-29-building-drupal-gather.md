As a previous organiser of PHP and Drupal meetups, I know how much work goes into running a meetup/user group.

Every event needs a venue, speakers, sponsors, food, drink, etc, as well as the ongoing responsibilities between events.

Running a website for a meetup group shouldn't be difficult.

Software as a solution platforms are expensive and suffer from feature bloat.

I don't want to see ads or promotions from random companies after registering for an event, or constant automated appeals to save a group that is soon to expire.

So, I'm building Drupal Gather - a distribution for meetup and user group websites.

Different to groups.drupal.org or PaaS solutions, this will be a distribution for a meetup organisers to manage their own group and events, and not a directory of multiple groups.

It will also be free and open source, and for any type of meetup - not just Drupal or PHP meetups.

This is in the very early stages of development, and I'm currently working [in my own monorepo](https://git.oliverdavies.uk/opdavies/drupal-gather) but will create separate projects and issue queues on Drupal.org for the example project, installation profile and recipes.

I intend to keep the amount of custom code to a minimum, and create a working MVP with basic features in the near future.

I'll be writing update posts as I develop it and may also [live stream some of it](https://www.youtube.com/@opdavies) if time allows.
